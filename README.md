# Norwalk Coastal Flood Explorer

An interactive coastal flooding visualization tool for **Norwalk, Connecticut**, developed using numerical hydrodynamic modeling and regional sea-level projections.

### Explore the interactive viewer

**[Launch the Norwalk Coastal Flood Explorer](https://joshcuzz.github.io/norwalk-coastal-explorer/?version=6751a34)**

The explorer includes three major components:

- **Tidal Flooding:** Modeled annual coastal flood frequency under 2024, 2050, and 2100 sea-level conditions, using 2024 astronomical tides.
- **Storm Flooding:** Hurricane Sandy (2012) simulations under historical and projected 2050, 2075, and 2100 relative sea levels, together with a provisional synthetic 100-year storm experiment.
- **Future Sea Level:** Potential ocean-connected inundation associated with rising mean higher high water (MHHW) levels.

### Modeling approach

Coastal flooding is simulated using **SFINCS**, with 20 m hydrodynamic modeling and high-resolution 2 m terrain information. Future sea-level conditions use regional projections developed at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.

Interactive maps allow users to explore flood extent, frequency, and modeled flood depths, including land-cover-specific displays.

### Important limitations

This is a research prototype intended to explore potential coastal flooding and sensitivity to future sea-level rise. Results are preliminary and should not be used for property-level flood-risk determinations, emergency planning, or regulatory purposes. Some modeled areas may exhibit persistent-water or drainage artifacts. The synthetic 100-year experiment is provisional and is not a locally validated return-period flood map.
